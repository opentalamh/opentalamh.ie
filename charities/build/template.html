How to read it

Each dot is one registered charity. Charities with a single purpose sit in the outer clusters. Charities that list two or more purposes sit in the middle, pulled toward each purpose they list, so a dot between Religion and Relief of poverty lists both. Hover or tap a dot to see the charity's own statement of its objects.

What about church schools?

What this does not show

Purposes are chosen by each charity when it registers, from the list in section 3 of the Charities Act 2009. Hospitals, care homes and housing bodies run by religious orders often register under health or community welfare, so a count of "advancement of religion" is a floor for church-linked charities, not a ceiling. It also says nothing about land or assets.
