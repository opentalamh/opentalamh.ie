# Contribute form (Tally) – setup notes

Create at tally.so (free plan: unlimited submissions, file uploads up to 10 MB each, email notifications).
Set notifications to opentalamh@proton.me. Keep every field optional except the main description so it stays flexible.

Fields:
1. What are you sending? (multiple choice, "Other" allowed): Folio or deed / Photo / Planning or other document / Missing or wrong place on the map / Tip / Other
2. Tell us about it (long text, required)
3. Where is it? (short text: townland, address, Eircode or a Google Maps link)
4. Upload files (file upload, multiple: PDFs, photos)
5. Folio number, if you have one (short text)
6. Who owns it, as far as you know? (short text)
7. How do you know? (long text)
8. Your name (optional) / Your email (optional)
9. Credit me by name? (Yes / No, keep me anonymous)

Then: Share → Embed → Standard, copy the code into contribute.qmd where marked.
Later: connect Tally to a Google Sheet or Airtable when you want a review queue.
