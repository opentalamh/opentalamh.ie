Dear Ms Falsey,
I refer to your letter of 30" October on behalf of the Committee of Public Accounts seeking further
information in regard to matters referred to in my letter to the Committee of 17'" October.
I noted in earlier correspondence to the Committee that the Minister for Education and Skills may,
under section 42 of the Residential Institutions Statutory Fund Act 2012, accept properties for
transfer to certain State bodies including the Minister and the Health Service Executive subject to
the consent of the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform. As noted in the update I provided
to the Committee on 17" October, the consent of the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform
has been sought for the acceptance of three properties under section 42. The properties in question
are:
e Former primary school at Ballyjamesduff, Co. Cavan (Property 13 on Appendix 2 of 17
October update);
e McAuley Centre, Kells, Co. Meath (Property 14 on Appendix 2 of 17 October update); and
e Former St. Patrick’s Primary School, Westport, Co. Mayo (Property 17 on Appendix 2 of
17 October update).
It is hoped that the consent of the Minister will be given shortly.

In regard to the provision of timelines for the transfers, the Department is making every effort to
ensure that processes are completed quickly. It will be apparent from the tables attaching to my
previous reply, that the transactions have multiple steps. The process of completing property
transfers involves a number of different parties, including the receiving State bodies (this
Department, the Health Service Executive and a local authority), the various transferring
congregations, legal representatives for each party, other State bodies such as the Charities
Regulatory Authority, the Property Registration Authority, etc. In the case of the above properties,
the next step, once Ministerial consent is obtained, is for the relevant congregations to seek the
approval of the Charities Regulatory Authority.
With respect to properties offered to the HSE, I have sought additional information from that
organisation. I have been assured also that it is prioritising the completion of all of the outstanding
transfers. _In_relation_to_the_National_Rehabilitation_Hospital,_I_am—informed—that—there—are
significant complexities arising in this case due to the trust-based legal structures involved. I have
been assured by the HSE that the matter is being prioritised. I note that the Committee intends to
write to the HSE about the matter also.
In the context of the foregoing, it will be apparent that the Department does not have executive
powers in respect of many of the matters bearing on the transfers. As such, it is necessary to provide
the Committee with our best estimates on completion dates, rather than fixed deadlines.
The Department will continue to liaise proactively with all relevant stakeholders in order to
progress the transfers and to identify any particular issues that may be delaying the completions.
I will continue to provide regular updates to the Committee and will apprise the committee in those
reports of all matters of significance relating to the transfers.
